    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, difficulty about 4 out of 10 — the guides agree the grind is long but forgiving. The story takes 60–80 hours, the 100% checklist about 100 hours, and cleanup about 10–20 hours. Follow the stages in order.

    Rules of the run

    • No cheats. Using a cheat disables trophies and saving, so keep them off for the whole run.
    • Keep manual saves. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so save by hand at the start and end of every chapter — there are 15 manual save slots.
    • Free roam after the story. Everything except one Chapter 1 meditation can still be done after the credits.
    • Scan and study as you go. WAiNK-scan every new vehicle and study animals through binoculars from the start — 200 vehicles and 100 animals are needed later.
    • One possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia; everything else can be cleaned up afterwards.
